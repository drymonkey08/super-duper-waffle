# Script 02 — "The Cost of Comfort"
**Niche:** Masculinity / Luxury Mindset
**Format:** Voiceover + Runway ML visuals
**Duration:** 45–60 seconds
**Hook Type:** Pain Point / Uncomfortable Truth

---

## VISUAL DIRECTION (Runway ML Prompts)

**Scene 1 (0–5s):** Man slumped on a couch in dim light, phone screen glow on his face. Slow zoom in. Hyperrealistic, cinematic.

**Scene 2 (5–20s):** Split screen — left side: man wasting time, scrolling phone in a cluttered apartment. Right side: empty gym at 4am, weights, silence.

**Scene 3 (20–35s):** Montage of luxury — penthouse view, private jet interior, a man in a tailored suit signing a document. Fast cuts, cinematic grade.

**Scene 4 (35–50s):** Man alone at a desk at night, laptop open, city lights behind him through floor-to-ceiling windows. Focused, determined.

**Scene 5 (50–60s):** Slow-motion champagne pour into a glass overlooking a rooftop infinity pool at sunset.

---

## VOICEOVER SCRIPT

> Comfort is the most expensive thing you'll ever buy.
>
> You pay for it with your potential.
>
> Every time you chose the couch over the gym — you paid.
>
> Every time you swiped instead of studied — you paid.
>
> Every time you said "tomorrow" — you paid.
>
> Comfort feels free. But the bill comes due.
>
> The men living the life you want?
>
> They paid a different price — in sweat, silence, and sacrifice.
>
> You don't get the view without climbing the mountain.
>
> So what are you paying for today?
